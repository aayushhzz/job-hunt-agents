# Candidate profile

Copy this file to `profile.md` and fill it in. Every agent reads `profile.md` each round,
so edit it whenever your situation changes. `profile.md` is git-ignored.

## Target

- The roles you want, the pay floor you're aiming for, and whether remote / contract / EOR is OK.

## Eligibility (hard rules: a job that fails these is not a job for this candidate)

- Where you live and your working time zone.
- Remote: which employers count (for example, ones that hire in your country).
- Onsite or hybrid: the cities you'd accept.

## Experience

- Years of experience, recent roles, and the level you map to.

## Strengths (production depth)

- The things you've shipped and debugged for real.

## Languages

- Programming languages, strongest first.

## Roles that fit

- Titles and team types to search for.

## Not interested in

- Roles, requirements or listings to skip.
